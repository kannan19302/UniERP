# Platform Admin OS Experience Requirements

- `PAO-UX-001`: Every view shall identify scope: provider estate, region, environment or tenant.
- `PAO-UX-002`: Unknown, stale, degraded and healthy states shall be visually and semantically distinct.
- `PAO-UX-003`: Destructive operations shall show target, blast radius, reversibility and required reason.
- `PAO-UX-004`: Tables and workflows shall meet WCAG 2.2 AA keyboard, focus, name and contrast requirements.
- `PAO-UX-005`: Dates, currencies, numbers and time zones shall be explicit and locale-aware.
- `PAO-UX-006`: Shared patterns shall come from `design-system`; platform pages shall not fork them locally.

The existing route breadth establishes information-architecture intent only. Usability and accessibility
conformance remain `UNVERIFIED` until tested against representative workflows.
