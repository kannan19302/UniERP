# Tenant Admin Experience

- `TAD-UX-001`: The active organization and environment shall remain visible.
- `TAD-UX-002`: Permission editors shall show effective access, inherited access and conflicts before save.
- `TAD-UX-003`: Security changes shall explain user impact, rollback and lockout risk.
- `TAD-UX-004`: Async/bulk operations shall show durable progress and recoverable failures.
- `TAD-UX-005`: All workflows shall use shared design-system tokens/components and meet WCAG 2.2 AA.
- `TAD-UX-006`: Locale, time zone, currency and date effects shall be previewable.
