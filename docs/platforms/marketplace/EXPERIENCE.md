# Marketplace UX and Design Requirements

Experience scope: trust signals, permissions-before-install, pricing clarity, compatibility warnings and reversible lifecycle actions.

- `MKT-UX-001`: Interfaces shall make actor, tenant/environment scope and lifecycle state explicit.
- `MKT-UX-002`: Loading, empty, stale, degraded, failed and successful states shall be distinct.
- `MKT-UX-003`: Destructive or irreversible actions shall disclose target, impact and recovery.
- `MKT-UX-004`: Shared patterns shall use the Design Platform and meet WCAG 2.2 AA.
- `MKT-UX-005`: Localization, time, number, currency and unit semantics shall be unambiguous where applicable.

UI presence is DECLARED evidence only until backed by real contracts and workflow tests.
