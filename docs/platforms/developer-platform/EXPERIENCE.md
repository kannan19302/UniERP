# Developer Platform UX and Design Requirements

Experience scope: copyable examples, deterministic validation, explicit scopes/quotas, deprecation guidance and accessible builder workflows.

- `DEV-UX-001`: Interfaces shall make actor, tenant/environment scope and lifecycle state explicit.
- `DEV-UX-002`: Loading, empty, stale, degraded, failed and successful states shall be distinct.
- `DEV-UX-003`: Destructive or irreversible actions shall disclose target, impact and recovery.
- `DEV-UX-004`: Shared patterns shall use the Design Platform and meet WCAG 2.2 AA.
- `DEV-UX-005`: Localization, time, number, currency and unit semantics shall be unambiguous where applicable.

UI presence is DECLARED evidence only until backed by real contracts and workflow tests.
