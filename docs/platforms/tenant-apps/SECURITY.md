# Tenant Apps ERP Security and Compliance

- `ERP-SEC-001`: Every operation shall authenticate a principal and enforce tenant, permission and record scope.
- `ERP-SEC-002`: Database isolation shall prevent tenant access even when application filters are missing.
- `ERP-SEC-003`: Segregation-of-duties conflicts shall be detectable for financial and approval workflows.
- `ERP-SEC-004`: Sensitive HR, health, payment and identity data shall have field/purpose-aware access and retention.
- `ERP-SEC-005`: Exports, reports, search and background jobs shall enforce source authorization.
- `ERP-SEC-006`: Business audit records shall be immutable and link reversals/amendments to originals.

Compliance scope depends on enabled modules and customer deployment; documents must record evidence and
applicability rather than claim blanket certification.
