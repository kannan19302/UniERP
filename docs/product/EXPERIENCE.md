# UniERP Product Experience Requirements

- `UNI-UX-001`: Every authenticated surface shall make platform, tenant/environment and user scope explicit.
- `UNI-UX-002`: Shared tokens, themes, density and components shall come from the Design Platform.
- `UNI-UX-003`: Lifecycle state, allowed action, validation, progress and recovery shall be visible.
- `UNI-UX-004`: Unknown/stale/degraded state shall never be presented as healthy or complete.
- `UNI-UX-005`: Critical workflows shall meet WCAG 2.2 AA and representative screen-reader/keyboard evidence.
- `UNI-UX-006`: Money, quantity, tax, dates and time zones shall be explicit and locale-aware.
- `UNI-UX-007`: Mobile/desktop parity shall be declared per workflow; unsupported behavior shall not be hidden.
- `UNI-UX-008`: Provider destructive operations shall show target, blast radius, reason and reversibility.

Platform experience documents own workflow-specific information architecture. The legacy UI brief is retained
only as evidence and cannot override current component or accessibility proof.
